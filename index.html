Our company

Give people their time back.

Cass Technologies Ltd is the independent company that owns and develops our apps. Our mission is to use AI to take the effort out of everyday tasks.

With Macros, a photo of your lunch becomes an estimate of its calories and nutrients. With Petey, a conversation about your goals, schedule and coaching style helps you find a personal trainer.

We design each app around the part it can take off your hands. Let Macros do the counting. Let Petey narrow the search. What you eat and who you train with are up to you.
