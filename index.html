Our company

Make the next step easier.

Cass Technologies Ltd owns and develops independent health and fitness apps. Our mission is to use advanced machine learning to make nutrition easier to understand and the right training support easier to find.

That work takes different forms. Macros uses image recognition to estimate calories and nutrients from a meal photo. Petey uses a conversation about your goals, schedule and coaching preferences to recommend personal trainers.

Both apps start with something you already do, whether that’s taking a picture or describing what you want. The technology does the counting or narrows the search. You take it from there.
